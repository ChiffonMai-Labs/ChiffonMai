# 版本说明（What's New）

## 简体中文

- 新增「删除账号」：可清除本机账号、成绩缓存与自动同步，并尽力删除服务端评论、评分与排行榜记录；未完成的项可一键邮件申请。
- 评论新增举报与屏蔽；被屏蔽用户的评论会从歌曲评论区与最近评论隐藏。
- 系统设置新增「已屏蔽用户」管理。
- 修复网络连接监听依赖缺失导致的构建问题。
- 修复部分界面文案与稳定性问题。

## English

- Added Delete Account: clears the local account, score cache and auto-sync, and deletes comments/ratings/ranking records where possible; remaining items can be requested by a pre-filled email.
- Added comment reporting and user blocking; blocked users' comments are hidden from chart and recent-comment lists.
- Added Blocked Users management in Settings.
- Fixed a missing connectivity dependency that broke builds.
- Assorted copy and stability fixes.
