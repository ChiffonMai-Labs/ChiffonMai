## Description

ChiffonMai is an unofficial companion toolkit for the arcade rhythm game maimai DX. It brings tools that players normally scatter across several websites into one app.

**Songs and charts**
Search by title, alias, artist, charter, version, type, difficulty and tags; view jacket art, constants, chart code and chart preview; import and play local charts; recognize jacket art with the camera or photo library; draw random songs by filters. Favorites keep practice lists and can be exported/imported between devices.

**Scores and Rating**
Browse full play records, Best50, Rating history and several leaderboards; calculate single-chart Rating and achievement, or reverse-calculate the judgments needed for a target; get recommendations by target Rating, constant range or tags; compare with friends; view tag-filtered Best50; export score and progress images.

**Arcade and casual**
Arcade map and maimai server status; an attendance wheel; background music player; several song-quiz modes and multiplayer quiz; update calendar and CN/JP version comparison.

**Sync and accounts**
Choose Diving Fish, LXNS or AWMC NET, with manual, multi-target and server-side scheduled sync; manage bindings and import credentials in one place.

**Other**
Four UI languages (Simplified/Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese); light/dark/pure-black themes, custom wallpaper and seed color; local backup; home widgets and in-app update (Android only).
