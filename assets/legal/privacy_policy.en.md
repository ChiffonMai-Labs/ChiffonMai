# ChiffonMai Privacy Policy

Updated: October 25, 2026

Effective: October 25, 2026

This Policy explains how ChiffonMai processes personal information when providing the application and associated online services. The developer and maintainer is ChiffonOwO, contactable at [chiffonowo@foxmail.com](mailto:chiffonowo@foxmail.com).

**Account authorization, automatic synchronization, leaderboard participation and public profiles are separate matters. Review each feature's choices and notices. Reading this Policy does not replace the authorization, public display choices or system permissions you grant for individual features.**

## 1. Information processed and purposes

### 1. Local features and preferences

Song caches, search history, favorites, calculator inputs, themes, export options and game settings are stored on your device for lookup, calculation, personalization and restoring your next session. Completing onboarding records the versions of the documents confirmed, confirmation time and whether a guardian confirmed them locally; no birth date or identity document is requested. Accessing online song data, covers, audio, charts, update information or web searches sends the necessary resource identifiers, search terms or version queries to the relevant service.

### 2. Account linking, score queries and synchronization

When you use these features, the app processes identifiers for the selected source, such as QQ number, friend code or player ID, and login tokens, refresh tokens, authorization codes or personal import keys. Results may include nickname, in-game appearance, song scores, achievement, DX score, Rating and score update times.

This information verifies identity, queries scores, switches accounts, displays statistics and performs score uploads you select. Login and import credentials use the device's system secure storage; retrieved scores and account display information are cached locally. The relevant authentication service processes information used for third-party login pages or QR authorization.

**Withholding credentials affects personal score queries or synchronization for that source, but not features that do not require its account authorization.**

### 3. Leaderboards and brief profiles

If you choose to participate and refresh data, the app submits account identifiers, data source, scores and statistics needed for rankings to the ChiffonMai server, to generate total Rating, song and other statistical leaderboards.

If nickname display is enabled, rankings show your nickname and community avatar according to your settings. If brief profile display is enabled, other users can open your row to see avatar, nickname, source, title, background, nameplate, app level, Best50/Best35/Best15 total Rating and the first entry in Best35/Best15.

When brief profile display is off, that drawer's content is not provided. Anonymous display uses an anonymous name and default avatar and hides the brief profile. **Anonymity is a public display setting; it does not mean the server stops processing identifiers and scores, or that all data has been anonymized.**

### 4. Community appearance, progression and interaction

Community avatars, titles, nameplates and backgrounds you choose and sync are associated with your account on the server for identity display in community features, rankings and multiplayer. After identity verification, automatic check-in on the first launch each day submits identifiers and credentials to record check-in dates, consecutive days, experience and app level.

For comments and ratings, the server processes account identifiers, nickname, submitted content and time for display, updating, deletion and necessary content management. Other users can view public content.

### 5. Multiplayer song guessing

Creating or joining a room processes room ID, player identifier, nickname, community avatar, ready status, guesses or revealed-letter records, rounds, scores and connection status on the server for room synchronization, deciding winners, settlement and reconnection. Other players see the nickname, avatar, guesses, scores and other information necessary for the game.

### 6. Automatic synchronization

**When enabled, the app submits the relevant account identifiers, game player IDs and synchronization credentials to the ChiffonMai server. The server encrypts managed credentials and reads, checks and uploads scores according to its task schedule. Tasks may continue after the app closes.**

Task records include status, time, number of items processed, results, errors and score snapshots, for queuing, avoiding duplicates, progress queries and troubleshooting. A management token is stored separately on your device to query, change or disable your tasks.

Disabling a task clears managed credentials and stops future execution. Completed score uploads are not withdrawn, and task status and completed statistics are not all deleted by disabling the switch. **Closing or uninstalling the app does not disable server tasks for you.**

### 7. Image recognition and translation

Cover recognition calculates features of your selected image on the device and matches them against cover data.

For score-photo recognition, the image you select and submit and the required sign-in token are sent to maimai Score Hub's recognition service. Visible nicknames, scores and other text in that image are included. You separately decide whether to upload recognized results to a score data source.

For translation, song titles or collectible descriptions and language parameters are forwarded through the ChiffonMai server to Baidu's translation service to obtain a translation. These images or texts are not submitted when you do not use the relevant feature.

### 8. Arcade maps

When you actively use location and grant permission, the app reads your current position to move the map and calculate arcade distances. The current map page does not upload precise location to the ChiffonMai server as an account profile.

Map tiles are requested for the area you browse, allowing the provider to know the requested map area. When you open external navigation, the selected arcade's destination information is passed to your chosen map application.

### 9. Operation and diagnostics

Online services process IP addresses, request paths, access times, response status and errors needed for communication, responding to requests, rate limiting, security and troubleshooting.

Recent diagnostic logs are kept locally. If you export them, the file includes app version, OS version, time and recent errors and operational records; records may contain account or song identifiers. Exporting does not automatically send the file to the maintainer. You decide whether to share it.

## 2. System permissions

Manage permissions in onboarding, relevant features or OS settings. Refusing an optional permission does not affect other features.

- **Photos, media or storage:** choose backgrounds, cover art and images for recognition, and save or access exports. Scope depends on your OS and authorization choice.
- **Camera:** take recognition images or scan QR codes for sign-in or authorization, only after you enter the relevant feature.
- **Location:** locate yourself on arcade maps and calculate distances. You can still browse maps manually without permission.
- **Notifications:** background music notifications and playback controls, not reading your notification content.
- **Network and background playback:** access online materials, sync data and maintain media playback you enable. Foreground services or playback controls shown by the system do not grant access to unrelated information.

Revoking permission stops the dependent feature from acquiring that information. It does not by itself delete information already saved.

## 3. Third-party services and components

The following services receive relevant information according to the features you use. Direct connections also disclose network information necessary for communication. Their websites provide service rules, privacy information and contact details.

- **[Diving-Fish](https://www.diving-fish.com/maimaidx/prober/), [LXNS](https://maimai.lxns.net/), [AWMC NET](https://net.wmc.pub/):** selected identifiers, authorization or import credentials, query conditions and scores to upload for verification, queries and sync.
- **[maimai Score Hub](https://maimai.bakapiano.com/):** account information, game player identifiers and login tokens required for QR sign-in and synchronization, and submitted score photos, for authorization, score reading or recognition.
- **[DXRating.net](https://dxrating.net/), [Union](https://union.godserver.cn/), [Yuri-YuzuChaN alias data](https://github.com/Yuri-YuzuChaN/maimaiDX):** requests for public songs, tags, aliases or covers. Your score-sync credentials are not submitted to obtain these public materials.
- **LXNS resource services and AWMC NET chart resource services:** cover, audio, appearance or chart identifiers to download and play materials.
- **[NearCade](https://nearcade.cn/) and [Amap](https://www.amap.com/) map resources:** arcade queries or requests for resources in the browsed area to provide arcade information and maps. External navigation uses the map app you choose.
- **[Baidu Translate](https://fanyi-api.baidu.com/):** text, source language and target language forwarded through our server for translation.
- **[Bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/):** search terms, video identifiers and corresponding requests when you search for videos or open related content. Its rules apply after you leave the app.
- **[isMaiDown](https://mai.chongxi.us/) and [status.awmc.cc](https://status.awmc.cc/status/maimai):** status queries to provide maimai server status.

Flutter system plugins implement secure storage, photo/file selection, photography and scanning, location, network state and media playback. They call system interfaces according to the feature in use; installing the app does not grant every permission. Secure storage uses flutter_secure_storage; image picking and scanning use image_picker, mobile_scanner and related components; location uses geolocator; playback uses just_audio, audio_service, audioplayers and related components.

Providers describe their server locations, retention periods and other processing rules. You may exercise rights with the provider or contact this app's maintainer for matters requiring coordination.

## 4. Retention and security

1. Local settings, caches, favorites and profiles support continued use. Clear them through app data management or the OS. Credentials in system secure storage follow OS storage and removal mechanisms.
2. Managed sync credentials remain until you disable the task or clear its managed configuration. Ranking scores remain until updated or deleted. Community profiles, progression, interactions and executed-task records remain while providing the service or are handled following verified deletion requests. This version does not set a single automatic expiry period for all these server records.
3. Clearing local caches, account profiles or uninstalling does not also delete server or third-party data. Each third party handles data it has received under its rules.
4. We use encrypted transport, system secure storage, encrypted managed credentials and access verification. For security incidents caused by failures, attacks or other reasons, we take remedial measures appropriate to the incident and fulfill notification and reporting duties required by law.
5. Exported images, favorites, charts and logs are device files saved where the app indicates. After sharing or sending them, the app does not control recipients' copies.

## 5. Disclosure and public availability

We submit information needed for each feature to its data source or provider. Public display follows your leaderboard, profile and community publication choices. We do not sell your personal information.

Except for processing you request or authorize or that is necessary to meet legal obligations, information is not provided to unrelated third parties. If service succession or a change of operator requires transferring personal information, we notify you of the recipient and its contact information as required by law. Changes to purposes or methods follow the applicable procedures.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Within applicable law, you may access, copy, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw authorization and request an explanation of this Policy.

- **Accounts and authorization:** manage accounts and credentials in the system section and revoke access on the relevant third-party platform. Deleting a local profile does not revoke third-party authorization.
- **Leave rankings or change display:** deselect leaderboard participation or turn off nickname/brief-profile display when refreshing, then refresh to submit the changes. Retry as directed if a request fails. Copies already saved by others are not withdrawn.
- **Stop managed sync:** disable the task on the Automatic sync page before clearing credentials or uninstalling, so you do not lose task management access.
- **Permissions:** revoke photo, camera, location or notification permissions in OS settings.
- **Delete server information:** email requests for community appearance, progression, comments, ratings or other information without self-service deletion. State the source, necessary identifier and request scope; do not send passwords, full tokens or login QR codes.

We verify account ownership in a manner appropriate to the request and respond within fifteen working days. If a request cannot be fulfilled or some information must lawfully be kept, we explain why and available next steps. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing completed before withdrawal.

## 7. Minors

The app is not directed at children under 14. Minors should use it under guardian guidance. Users under 14 must not link accounts, upload scores or publish personal information without guardian consent. Guardians who discover information processed without consent may contact the maintainer to request verification and deletion.
