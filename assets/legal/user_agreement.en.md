## 2. Accounts and authorization

1. Browsing public song libraries, using calculators and most local features do not require a third-party account. Personal score queries, score synchronization and community posting require the relevant identity or authorization credentials.
2. Use your own account or one you are lawfully authorized to use. Do not submit unauthorized account passwords, tokens, import keys, login QR codes or other people's personal information.
3. **Sign-in authorization, score queries and score uploads are separate actions. Synchronization to another data source follows your choices in the relevant feature.** A third-party platform's rules govern updates, deletion and account management for scores it has received.
4. Keep sign-in credentials and task management tokens secure. If credentials leak, an abnormal sign-in occurs or synchronization is unauthorized, promptly revoke third-party authorization, replace the affected credentials and contact the maintainer.

## 3. Automatic synchronization and public display

1. Once enabled, automatic synchronization runs on the server and does not require the app to remain running or your phone to stay online. Execution depends on data source availability, API limits and task queues.
2. **Closing the app, deleting a local account profile or uninstalling the app does not automatically disable server-side synchronization tasks. To stop managed synchronization, first disable the relevant task on the Automatic sync page.** Subsequent scheduled runs stop after disabling; scores already submitted to a data source are not withdrawn.
3. You separately choose leaderboard participation, nickname display and brief profile display when refreshing data. Information you permit to be public can be viewed by other users; the Privacy Policy describes its scope.
4. Showing an anonymous nickname does not mean the server anonymizes account data. Account identifiers are still needed to maintain scores, ranks and display settings. Content other users have already saved or exported is not automatically withdrawn when you later change your settings.
5. Your selected community avatar, title, nameplate and background are associated with the relevant account and used according to the display settings of the relevant features.
